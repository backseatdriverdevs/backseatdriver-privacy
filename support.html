Common Questions

How do I add or remove favourite series?

Tap "ADD" on the home screen to choose new series, or use the "REORDER" option to rearrange them. To remove a series, tap into it and toggle the star icon in the top right.

How do I change session reminder times?

Go to Settings → Notifications. You can pick multiple reminder intervals (5, 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes before each session).

Why don't I see notifications on Android?

Make sure notifications are enabled in your device's system settings for Backseat Driver, and that you've enabled them inside the app under Settings → Notifications.

The app says "no upcoming sessions" — what's wrong?

Pull down on the home screen to refresh. If the issue persists, try restarting the app. The schedule cache refreshes periodically.
